ShootSpot helps photographers and filmmakers find and share shooting locations. Each location is a photo, a pin on the map and practical notes: light, access, permits, parking. Locations are anonymous unless you choose to show your name.

## Sign in

1. Open ShootSpot and enter your email address.
2. We email you a 6-digit code. Enter it in the app. There is no password.
3. If the code doesn't arrive, check your spam folder or tap _Send a new code_.

The first time you sign in, a short onboarding asks for your name and for you to accept the [Terms of use](/shootspot/legal/terms/), the [Privacy notice](/shootspot/legal/privacy/) and the [Image licence](/shootspot/legal/image-licence/).

## Browse locations

The bar at the bottom has three tabs: **Feed**, **Map** and **Profile**, plus the camera button.

- **Feed** shows locations as a photo grid. The scope chip at the top switches between _Everywhere_, _Nearby_ and a place you search for. The filter sheet narrows the results by radius and tags.
- **Map** shows the same locations as pins. Zoom in to split clusters, and tap a pin for a preview.
- Tap a location to open it: the photo, the light and direction it was shot in, access and practical notes, and _Open in Maps_ for directions.

## Capture and publish a location

1. Tap the camera button and take a photo. You can also tap **+** and choose _Import photos from your gallery_.
2. **Confirm the location.** The pin uses your GPS position or the photo's own location. You can search for a place or move the pin by hand. Location access is optional.
3. **Add notes.** Name the place, then add what helps others plan a shoot: best light, access, permits, parking, tripod and drone rules, hazards.
4. **Publish.** The location is anonymous unless you turn on _Show my name on this location_. You can change that later.

No connection? The location is saved on your phone and sent automatically when you're back online.

All photo metadata (GPS tags, camera serial numbers) is removed before a photo is published, and published photos get a gentle automatic tone balance.

## Likes and projects

- **Like** a location with the heart. Likes are private: only you see them, in _Profile → Liked_.
- **Projects** are private lists for a shoot or a client. On a location, tap _Save to a project_, or create a new project there. Manage your projects in _Profile → Projects_.
- Long-press a liked location to add it to a project or remove it from Liked.

## Your locations

_Profile → Locations_ lists everything you have published. Open one of your locations to switch your name on or off, or delete it.

## Report a location

If a location shows a private home, a place that can only be reached by trespassing, or anything else that breaks the [Terms of use](/shootspot/legal/terms/), open it and tap _Report this location_.

## Settings

Open _Profile → menu → Settings_ to edit your profile, request a copy of your data, or [delete your account](/shootspot/legal/delete-account/).
